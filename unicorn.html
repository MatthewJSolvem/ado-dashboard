Unicorn

Protects the sprint from daily churn. It separates planned from unplanned work, shows exactly when scope was added, and turns "P1s, fast-tracked and unrecorded work" into numbers you can take to stakeholders. Built on the five ideals of The Unicorn Project: protect flow, record daily reality, stay blameless, isolate the chaos, and defend the work that matters to customers.
